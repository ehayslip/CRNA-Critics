Delete your CRNA Critics account

Works the same on crnacritics.com and in the CRNA Critics app for iPhone and Android. Operated by Medcrisis Anesthesia LLC.

Delete it yourself (takes about a minute)

  1. Sign in at crnacritics.com or open the app.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of any page and tap Delete my profile in the footer.
  3. Choose what happens to your reviews. Tick Also delete my reviews to remove every review and rating you posted. Leave it unticked and your reviews stay on the site, still anonymous, but permanently disconnected from you: you will no longer be able to edit or delete them.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, phone number, NBCRNA number, and password.
  • Your sign-in sessions and any saved contact preferences.
  • Your reviews and ratings, if you chose to remove them.

What may be kept

  • Reviews you chose to keep up, with nothing linking them to you.
  • A record that the Terms of Use were accepted (version, date, and the IP address and browser used at the time), kept as long as needed to document that agreement.
  • Server access logs, kept for a limited time for security, and routine backups, which age out on their own schedule.
  • Anything we are required by law to retain.

Delete only some of your data

You do not have to close your account to remove content. From My reviews you can edit or delete any individual review at any time. You can also unsubscribe from marketing email with the link in any message, or reply STOP to end text messages.

Prefer to ask us?

Email eric@crnacritics.com from the address on your account and say whether you want your reviews removed or kept. We will delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.
